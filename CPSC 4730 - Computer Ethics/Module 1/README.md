## M1: Assignment: Ethical Scenario

### CPSC 4733: Computer Ethics

#### Instructions
As you learn about different ethical principles and theories in this course, it is important to think about how you might apply what you are learning in different real-world situations. Respond to the prompt below in a short paper.

#### Prompt:
From either a **Deontological** or a **Consequentialist** perspective, make a case for or against the necessity of always telling the truth.

#### Requirements
- The paper will be graded on both content and form (correct grammar, punctuation, etc.).
- The paper should make a case for or against some position.
  - Do not say both sides have merit, or it is difficult to determine which side is right, or explore both sides equally.
  - You need to tell us why one side is better than the other.
- The paper should be a minimum of 300 words long.
- Be sure to include any bibliographic references and footnotes using ACM format.
- Submit the paper in either MS Word (.doc, .docx) or PDF (.pdf) format.
- Upload the file through this page by the posted deadline.
- Turnitin will be enabled for the assignment.
